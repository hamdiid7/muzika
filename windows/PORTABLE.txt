Muzika for Windows

If you downloaded the single-file Muzika.exe, open it directly. It prepares
its required app files in your local app folder, then opens Muzika.
Your library is preserved when you open a newer build.

Extract the entire ZIP to a folder, then open Muzika.exe.
Keep the data folder and all DLLs beside the executable.
If Windows reports a missing VCRUNTIME or MSVCP DLL, install Microsoft's
Visual C++ x64 runtime: https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe

This Windows version uses the same Flutter screens, themes, library,
search, lyrics, sharing cards and mini player as the Android app.
The Android audio equalizer and built-in Psiphon tunnel are not available
on Windows. Direct connections and manual proxies are supported.

Your library and settings are stored separately from the Android app.
To match libraries, open Library > Sync library (the QR button).
On the updated Android app, open the same page, scan the Windows QR code,
and tap Sync both libraries. Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi.
If Windows Firewall asks, allow Muzika on your private network.
The code expires after five minutes. Missing items are added in both directions:
liked songs, playlists, albums, followed artists, folders, pins, radio favourites
and recently played. Playlists with the same name combine their songs.
Nothing is removed. Downloaded audio files themselves are not copied.
Settings > Import/export is also available for transferring app data.
